Service Command Center
Mission Account Perception Evaluation Funnel System
Before the upload

Your customer file can be perceived in more than one useful way.

Before you enter a customer.xls file, Service Command Center helps you understand what the file may become: a route map, a cleanup queue, a survey list, and a growth signal.

Operational

Who needs attention?

Accounts can be sorted into ready, pending, problem, opportunity, and manager-review groups.

Territory

Where does work cluster?

Accounts can be viewed by route group, city, service area, technician, or quarter.

Relationship

Who represents multiple accounts?

Shared emails can reveal property managers, multi-location contacts, and outreach risks.

Cleanup

What is missing?

Missing emails, addresses, phones, unclear locations, and duplicates become a review list.
